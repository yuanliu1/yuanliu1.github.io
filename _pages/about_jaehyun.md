#### Jaehyun Lee

Ph.D. Student, Computer Science, NC State

Jaehyun Lee is a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at North Carolina State University, where his research focuses on quantum optimization algorithms for hybrid oscillator-qubit architectures. His work explores how continuous-variable and discrete-variable quantum systems can be combined to develop more efficient algorithms for solving optimization problems, with an emphasis on bridging theoretical algorithm design with the practical constraints of near-term quantum hardware. He is also interested in molecular simulation and compilation on CV-DV quantum processors. [Email](mailto:jlee283@ncsu.edu) [Website](https://jaehyunslee.github.io)
