#### Yongxiang Lyu

Ph.D. Student, Electrical and Computer Engineering, NC State (co-advise with Dr. Huiyang Zhou)

Yongxiang received his B.S. in Communication Engineering from Tianjin University in 2022, followed by dual M.S. degrees in ECE from the Georgia Institute of Technology and in EE from Tianjin University in 2025. His research interests lie at the intersection of AI and computing systems, with a primary focus on AI-assisted Hamiltonian simulation and fault-tolerant algorithms. He is also interested in efficient computing architectures, including LLM inference acceleration and hardware software co-design for VLA models.
