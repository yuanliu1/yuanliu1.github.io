#### Dr. Jingchen Zhang

Postdoctoral Scholar at NC State

Jingchen earned his Ph.D. in Chemical Physics from the University of Maryland, College Park in May 2026, advised by Yu Liu and Paul Julienne, with a focus on the quantum control of ultracold molecules and collisions. He holds a B.S. in Physics with highest honor from Jilin University (2017) and an M.S. in Physics from the University of Pittsburgh (2020). His research explores the quantum information aspects of molecular qudits. [Email](mailto:jzhan284@ncsu.edu)
