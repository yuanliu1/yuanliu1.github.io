Our work [Robust Quantum Algorithmic Binary Decision-Making on Displacement Signals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16081) is now on arXiv!
We developed a new quantum detection protocol that can perform single-shot detection on arbitrary features of Gaussian signals on bosonic modes. We also discovered connections between frame theory and these quantum sensing protocols.
