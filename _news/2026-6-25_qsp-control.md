Our [Analytic Approach to Quantum Control Using Quantum Signal Processing](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26085) is now on arXiv, led by PhD student Aishwarya Majumdar!
In collaboration with John Martyn (PNNL & Harvard) and Nathan Wiebe (U Toronto/PNNL), we introduce QSP-Control, an analytic framework that brings the rigorous design principles of quantum signal processing into quantum control, enabling systematic pulse constructions for qubit-oscillator systems and precise manipulation of Fock states.
